Finish your degree without wasting time or money.

The problem

Guessing is what costs you.

Lost credits

You take a class, then the school says it does not count. You pay for it twice.

Wrong order

You take classes in the wrong order and add months to your finish date.

Wrong school

You enroll first, then find out the school requires a lot of unnecessary discussion questions and limits how fast you can complete courses.

How it works

Plan first. Then enroll.

We call this the Transfer First Strategy. You earn cheap credit up front, then finish the rest at a school that moves at your speed.

1

Pick the degree and the school

We look at your goal, your time, and your money. Then we compare self-paced and competency-based schools like UMPI YourPace, WGU, Capella FlexPath, East Texas A&M, University of Wisconsin Flexible Option, and UMass Global MyPath. See how each school works.

2

Stack credit before you enroll

You use lower cost providers like Sophia Learning, Study.com, CLEP, and DSST for courses your school has accepted before. This is the part that saves the most time and money.

Your work and life experience matters here. If you already do the work every day, that subject moves fast for you.

3

Follow your course order

You get a written Blueprint that shows what to take, in what order, and about how long each part should take. We walk you through the tools and resources that help you finish each course faster.

4

Enroll and finish

You send your credit to the school, start your remaining courses, and work the plan until you graduate.

Straight talk

Fast is possible. Easy is not.

A faster degree still means real work. You have to study, take tests, and keep moving every week. Some people finish in 6 months. Some take longer. It depends on your credits, your degree, your school, and how much time you put in.

We do not give credit and we do not give degrees. Schools do that. What we give you is a clear plan, a way to use what you already know from work and life, and the resources that help you move through courses faster.
