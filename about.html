About

We remove the guesswork before you spend a dollar on tuition.

GraduateCollegeFast is a coaching and planning service for adults going back to school. We do not sell classes. We sell a clear path.

The method

Transfer First.

Front load the cheap credit. Then finish the rest at a school that lets you move at your own speed.

Step one: alternative credit

Providers like Sophia Learning, Study.com, CLEP, and DSST cost a fraction of tuition. When your school has accepted those courses before, you can knock out a large chunk of your degree up front.

Your work and life experience helps here. Subjects you already handle at your job move much faster than subjects that are brand new to you.

Step two: a competency-based school

Schools like UMPI YourPace, WGU, Capella FlexPath, East Texas A&M, University of Wisconsin Flexible Option, and UMass Global MyPath let you move as fast as you can prove what you know. We compare them and pick the best fit for your situation.

We also walk you through the tools and resources that help you finish each course faster.

School rules, prices, and transfer policies change. Always confirm the current policy with the school before you enroll.

Honest limits

What we do not do.

We are not a college and we are not an accrediting body. We do not give out credit or degrees.

We do not guarantee that a school will accept your credit. That decision belongs to the school, every time.

We do not guarantee that you will graduate in a set number of months.

We do not replace your school advisor or program mentor.

We do not give financial aid or licensure advice. For financial aid questions, go to studentaid.gov.

We do not promise a job or a salary.

If you are looking at a licensed field like nursing, social work, counseling, or teaching, the rules are stricter and they change by state. We will tell you what to check, but you must confirm the requirements with the school and your state board.
