Schools

The schools we plan around most.

These schools let you move at your own speed. Each one works a little differently. The right one depends on your degree, your credits, and how you like to work.

UMPI YourPace

University of Maine at Presque Isle

How it works: Fully online with no set class times and no weekly deadlines. You move through courses by showing you know the material. You pay one flat rate for each 8-week session, no matter how many courses you finish. UMPI has no set limit on how many credits you can transfer in.

Best fit: Adults with past college credit or work experience who want to transfer in as much as possible.

Visit the official site

WGU

Western Governors University

How it works: Every bachelor's and master's degree at WGU is competency-based. You pay one flat rate for each 6-month term and can finish as many courses as you are able in that time. You still read, write papers, and take tests.

Best fit: Self-driven students who want longer terms and room to take on a lot of courses at once.

Visit the official site

Capella FlexPath

Capella University

How it works: You set your own deadlines. You can take up to two courses at a time, and each course must be finished within 12 weeks. You pay one tuition fee for each 12-week billing session. FlexPath is offered in select programs. Capella also accepts Sophia general education courses toward its bachelor's degrees.

Best fit: Students who want freedom inside a clear 12-week window.

Visit the official site

Purdue Global ExcelTrack

Purdue University Global

How it works: Courses are broken into small 1-credit pieces, each covering one skill. You take assessments to show what you already know and move past it. Tuition is a flat rate for each term.

Best fit: Disciplined, self-motivated students with strong knowledge from their job.

Visit the official site

UMass Global MyPath

University of Massachusetts Global

How it works: Fully online with no fixed class times. You work at your own pace inside a 24-week term and pay one set fee for that term. You can start throughout the year once you are admitted. A growing number of programs are offered this way.

Best fit: Independent learners who want a long term to work inside.

Visit the official site

East Texas A&M CBE

East Texas A&M University

How it works: Competency-based programs run in 7-week terms with a fixed tuition rate that includes digital textbooks. You start each term with up to two courses and can add more once you finish them.

Best fit: Students who like short terms and clear checkpoints.

Visit the official site

University of Wisconsin Flexible Option

Universities of Wisconsin

How it works: You work in 12-week subscription periods, and new periods start every month. There are no academic deadlines and no group work. You pass assessments when you are ready, then move on.

Best fit: Students who want to start any month and work without deadlines.

Visit the official site

Walden Tempo Learning

Walden University

How it works: Tempo Learning is competency-based and billed by 3-month subscription periods. There are no set weekly deadlines. You pass assessments, often case studies, to show what you know. It is offered in select programs.

Best fit: Students who want to use their work experience and set their own speed.

Visit the official site

We do not list prices here because tuition changes. School rules, programs, and transfer policies change too. Always confirm the current details on the school's official site before you enroll. Last checked September 2026.
