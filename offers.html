Ask me

Questions

Straight answers.

Are you a college? Do you give credit or degrees?

No. GraduateCollegeFast is a coaching and degree-planning service. We do not give credit and we do not give degrees. Schools do that. We give you a clear plan and help you follow it.

Can I really finish in 6 months?

Some people do. Some take longer. It depends on how many credits you already have, which degree you pick, which school you enroll in, and how many hours a week you can study. Your work and life experience helps too, because subjects you already know move faster. We build the plan to be as fast as your situation allows, and we tell you the honest timeline up front.

Do I have to quit my job?

No. The plan is built for working adults. You use self-paced courses and schools that let you move at your own speed. You still need to set aside study time every week.

Will all my credits transfer?

No one can promise that. We build plans around courses and providers that your target school has accepted before. The school still makes the final call on every credit.

What are Sophia Learning and Study.com?

They are lower cost online course providers. Many schools accept their courses for transfer credit. You take these courses before you enroll, so you start school with credit already done. Your plan only uses courses your target school has accepted before. The school still makes the final call.

Is the degree accredited?

The degree comes from an accredited university, not from us. We only plan around accredited schools. You should always confirm a school's current accreditation yourself before you enroll. We are really good at getting it right.

Do you take financial aid?

No. Our plans are paid directly. Whether you qualify for aid at your school is between you and the school. Start at studentaid.gov for aid questions.

What about nursing or other licensed fields?

Those paths have extra rules that change by state, including clinical placement and licensure requirements. We can help you plan to speed up your prerequisites. You must confirm the rules with the school and your state board before you commit.

What do I get first after I join?

You start with the Custom Blueprint intake inside your dashboard. Once you finish it, we build your personal course Blueprint and email it to you within 3 to 5 business days.
